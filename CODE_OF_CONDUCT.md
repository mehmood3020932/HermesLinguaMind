# Code of Conduct

Hermes LinguaMind is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community.

Participants must:

- be respectful and constructive;
- welcome different backgrounds and levels of technical experience;
- focus criticism on ideas and code, not people;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, or sexualized conduct;
- respect project licensing and privacy rules.

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or restrict participation when behavior violates this policy.

Report serious conduct concerns privately to the maintainers listed in the repository security/contact guidance rather than posting sensitive information publicly.
