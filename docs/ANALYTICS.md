# Privacy-Conscious Analytics Taxonomy

Only collect events that are necessary for product improvement and disclose them in the privacy policy.

Recommended events:

- `onboarding_completed`
- `learning_goal_selected`
- `lesson_started`
- `lesson_completed`
- `speaking_session_started`
- `speaking_session_completed`
- `correction_shown`
- `correction_repeated`
- `pronunciation_attempted`
- `streak_day_completed`
- `referral_shared`
- `referral_opened`
- `subscription_started`
- `subscription_cancelled`

Do not log raw microphone audio, access tokens, passwords, or unnecessary personal information in normal analytics events.
