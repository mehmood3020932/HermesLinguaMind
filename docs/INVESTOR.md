# Product & Traction Narrative

Hermes should be presented as a product with measurable learning outcomes, not as a collection of AI features.

## Core thesis

Human conversation is one of the most valuable parts of language learning, but private tutoring is expensive and many learners lack a comfortable practice partner. Hermes aims to provide an always-available, personalized conversation tutor with native-language explanations.

## Product wedge

**Native-language explanations + target-language conversation + voice + companion experience.**

## Metrics that matter

- activation rate;
- daily/weekly active learners;
- D7/D30 retention;
- minutes spoken per learner;
- lessons completed;
- pronunciation improvement;
- target-language speaking confidence;
- free-to-paid conversion;
- acquisition cost and referral rate.

GitHub stars and contributors are useful community signals, but they should not substitute for learner outcomes and retention.
