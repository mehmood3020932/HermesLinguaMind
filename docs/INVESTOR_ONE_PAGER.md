# Hermes LinguaMind — Investor One-Pager

## Vision
Make high-quality language practice as accessible as having a patient, always-available tutor who can explain concepts in the learner's own language.

## Product
Hermes is an open-source, voice-first AI language-learning platform. A learner selects a native/explanation language, a target language and a level. Hermes can explain difficult concepts in the native language, practice in the target language, listen to speech, provide feedback, remember learning signals and optionally present the experience through an AI companion/avatar.

## Why now
Speech models, local inference, open-source LLMs and generative media make conversational tutoring substantially more accessible to small teams. The product thesis is to combine those primitives into a coherent learning loop rather than expose a collection of AI demos.

## Wedge
**Native-language explanation + target-language conversation + voice feedback + personalization.**

## Moat to build
- Learner-specific memory and progression data
- High-quality curriculum and correction policies
- Language-pair coverage and community language packs
- Voice/pronunciation evaluation loops
- Open-source ecosystem and integrations
- Companion/avatar presentation layer

## Business model
- Free core experience to maximize acquisition
- Premium subscription for higher usage, advanced practice, premium companion capabilities and deeper analytics
- Education/institutional plans
- Future developer/API offerings where licensing permits

## North-star metric
**Meaningful speaking minutes per active learner per week**, paired with learning-outcome quality and retention.

## Metrics to report before fundraising
- Activation rate
- D1/D7/D30 retention
- Weekly active learners
- Speaking minutes per learner
- Lessons completed and mastered
- Pronunciation improvement
- Referral rate
- Free-to-paid conversion
- Gross margin / inference cost per active learner
- CAC and payback period
- GitHub contributors and community growth

Do not manufacture traction. Replace every placeholder with measured data from the product. A polished repository is a credibility layer; retention and learning outcomes are the investment evidence.
