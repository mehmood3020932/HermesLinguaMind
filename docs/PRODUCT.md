# Product Experience Contract

## First-run experience

The first session must answer three questions quickly:

1. What language do I speak?
2. What language do I want to learn?
3. What do I want to accomplish?

The user should reach a speaking interaction within a few minutes, not navigate a maze of settings.

## Tutor behavior

Hermes should:
- explain difficult material in the chosen explanation/native language;
- practice primarily in the target language;
- adjust vocabulary and grammar to level;
- correct selectively and clearly;
- encourage without pretending mistakes are correct;
- avoid fabricating progress or capabilities;
- retain only the learner data the deployment policy allows.

## Avatar behavior

The avatar is presentation, not the intelligence layer. The same tutor should work without an avatar. Avatar states should include idle, listening, thinking, speaking, success, correction and error/reconnect.

Real inference must be explicitly configured and licensed. A mock/demo path must be visually and technically distinguishable during development.

## Accessibility

- captions/transcript available;
- text input fallback;
- reduced-motion support;
- readable contrast;
- screen-reader labels;
- clear network/retry states;
- no essential action hidden behind animation.
